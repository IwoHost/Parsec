**PARSEC Initiative** — a terminal management game about running a shady organization that sends operatives into the Backrooms.

You're the director. Your job is to recruit expendable agents, pack them into teams, and dispatch them into the endless fluorescent nightmare — Level 0 carpet rooms, flooded hotels, pipe dreams, the works. They come back (mostly) with almond water, salvage, and data. Sometimes they come back injured. Sometimes they don't come back at all.

Manage your roster's stress and grit. Research cursed tech. Upgrade your facility. Study anomalies you wish you hadn't found. Chase contracts from clients you've never met. And when your operation finally collapses under its own entropy — Fragment. Reset. Go deeper.

Pure ASCII. Runs in your terminal. Saves to `~/.hostlab/parsec.json`.

**Controls:** `D` dispatch · `H` hire · `R` research · `F` facility · `S` save · `Q` quit
